Ludo

Roll a 6 to leave your base. Land on an opponent to send them home, unless the square is a star or a start square. You need the exact number to reach the centre. A 6, a capture, or reaching the centre gives another roll. Pass and play: set two or more colors to Human and share one device. Online: tap Host online game, set seats to Open seat, and send the code to people who can open this page with Contributor access; they join with the code and tap an open seat.
